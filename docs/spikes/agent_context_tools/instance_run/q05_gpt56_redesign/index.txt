  What does Apple say its biggest business risks are, how concentrated is revenue by product and by geography,
  and does the desk hold any of that as figures?

Why this one: of the twenty, it is the only question that shares no component with what the Q07 run exercised
(Jaccard distance 1.00; selection.py / selection.txt). Q07 was the lead alone on analyze, formulas and book
columns, ranked and compared, over a book sector. Q05 is a single issuer, a delegation to the issuer analyst,
filing text through filings_search and filings_section, passage validation, and a question about what the desk
does not hold.

What the run showed (observer_repro.txt reproduces each point read-only)
  1. A dollar figure with a scale word is never checked. The answer tokenizer yields '$142.263 billion' as one
     number; the observer's parser accepts bare numbers only, returns None, and the observer skips the token.
     20 of 20 such figures in the analyst's note and 12 of 12 in the answer were skipped. The answer's verdict
     reads 0 figures, it carries no citations, and it was delivered as answered.
  2. Passage support is a substring search over every passage of the turn. 9 of the 10 growth rates the note's
     verdict called supported matched unrelated text (tax rates, a deferred-revenue schedule, R&D as a share of
     sales). The one whose true source is in the ledger, Greater China 33%, was called unsourced: the ledger
     keeps that row as '46023\xa034515\xa033\xa0%', without the thousands separators the model read and with a
     no-break space before the percent sign.
  3. Completion is 'unknown' whenever a turn has no analyze view, so the three parts of this question were never
     checked and the delivery stands at answered.
  4. The handbook says segment, product and geographic concentration figures are quoted, "never derived from
     parts" (analytics/handbook.py, the issuer chapter's absent list), and no verb turns a filing-table figure
     into a typed fact the calculator can use. So "how concentrated" was answered without the shares:
     iPhone 55.8% and the Americas 40.6% of six-month net sales.
  5. Smaller: the analyst spent its 12 reads on search snippets and never opened a section; the budget-exhausted
     request sent the tool list [open] alone, so its 15,987-token prompt had 0 cached tokens; the lead's
     llm_call steps carry no actor (the Q07 runs show the same).
